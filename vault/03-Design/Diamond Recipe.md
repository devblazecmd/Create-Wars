# Diamond Recipe

**Script:** `pack/kubejs/server_scripts/diamond_compacting.js`

## Recipe
Create compacting, **superheated**: 9 × Block of Coal → 1 × Diamond
(Mechanical Press over a Basin on a Blaze Burner fed blaze cake.)

## Why
No mod in the pack produces diamonds. [[../01-Mods/Create|Create]] only crushes diamond ore, and Create
Crafts & Additions only turns diamonds into grit. Added 2026-11-21 at the project owner's request.

## Balance notes
- 9 coal blocks (81 coal) per diamond. Superheated gating needs blaze cake, so this is a mid-game
  automation recipe, not an early shortcut.
- Not yet balance-tested in PvP. If diamond gear floods servers, raise the coal count or add a
  sequenced-assembly step.
