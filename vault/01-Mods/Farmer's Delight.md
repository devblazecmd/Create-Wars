# Farmer's Delight

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight)

## Role in Create Wars
The pack's food mod (owner request, 2026-11-21: "add a food mod"). It adds new crops (tomato, cabbage,
onion, rice), knives, the cutting board, stove, skillet, cooking pot, and meals that give the Nourishment
and Comfort effects. Feasts are placed blocks that serve several plates, which works well for team
play before a raid.

Chosen because it's the standard food base, and it ships its own Create integration: milling recipes
for wild crops under `data/farmersdelight/recipe/integration/create/`. [[Create Slice and Dice]] adds
the automation on top.

## Dependencies
- Hard requires: NeoForge `[21.1.219,)` (pack is on 21.1.267)
- Optional integration used by: [[Create Slice and Dice]]
