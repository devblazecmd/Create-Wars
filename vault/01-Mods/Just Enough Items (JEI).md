# Just Enough Items (JEI)

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei)

## Role in Create Wars
Recipe/usage viewer. Essential once this many mods (40+) are adding items and recipes — without it,
figuring out how to craft anything from [[Create Big Cannons]] ammo to [[KubeJS]]-scripted custom recipes
becomes guesswork. Also the integration point [[KubeJS]] hooks into for showing custom recipes in-game.
