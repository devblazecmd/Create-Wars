# Trigger Mobs

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/trigger-mobs)

## Role in Create Wars
Lets mobs (and guard-type mobs) spawn with and fire NTGL/[[Create Gunsmithing]] guns. This is the main PvE "war" lever - mobs shooting back changes base defense and vehicle play much more than raw stat buffs.

## Dependencies
- Hard requires: [[NTGL NukaTeam's Gun Lib|NTGL]]

## Version notes
Added via `packwiz modrinth add trigger-mobs` on 2026-11-20; resolved to `1.5.6`.

## Known issues
Stacks with [[Improved Mobs]] (gear/stat scaling) and [[Enhanced AI]] (skeleton sniping) - three mods all making ranged mobs nastier. Needs a balance pass; see [[../03-Design/README|design docs]].
