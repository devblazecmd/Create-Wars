# Create Aeronautics: Automated Logistics

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-aeronautics-automated-logistics)

## Role in Create Wars
Airship station networks, automated docking, and repeatable cargo routes — the air equivalent of train
schedules. Gives factions supply lines that can be raided or defended, which ties storage into the war
loop rather than being purely base infrastructure.
