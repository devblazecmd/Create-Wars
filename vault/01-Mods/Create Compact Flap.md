**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-compact-flap)

## Role in Create Wars
Addon for [[Create Aeronautics]] adding the Compact Flap Bearing (combines Torsion Spring + Directional
Gearshift functionality) plus analog/physical variants. Lets aircraft/mechanism builds stay more compact —
QoL for vehicle building rather than new combat capability.
