# Create: Enchantment Industry

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-enchantment-industry)

## Role in Create Wars
Automated enchanting with Create machines: liquid experience, mechanical enchanting/disenchanting and
book copying. Lets factions mass-produce enchanted gear, which matters for PvP balance.

## Dependencies
- Hard requires: [[Create]] `>=6.0.10`, [[Create Dragons Plus|Create: Dragons Plus]] `>=1.11.9`
- Soft/optional compat with: [[Sable]] (in pack), Apothic Enchanting/Apotheosis/Apothic Spawners, Touhou
  Little Maid (not in pack)
