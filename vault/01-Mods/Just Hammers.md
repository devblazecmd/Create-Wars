# Just Hammers

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/just-hammers)

## Role in Create Wars
Adds hammers that mine in area patterns (3x3, 3x3x3, 5x5x1, 5x5x3, 5x5x5). Mining/building QoL —
speeds up terrain prep for bases, trenches, and large Create contraption footprints. No combat role.
