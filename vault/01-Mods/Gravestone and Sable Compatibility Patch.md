# Gravestone and Sable Compatibility Patch

## Role in Create Wars
Dying on a moving [[Sable]] sub-level (e.g. a [[Create Aeronautics]] airship) puts the grave **on the ship's deck**. Without it, the grave lands in the world, clips into the ship's collision shape and stalls its physics.

## Dependencies
- Hard requires: [[GraveStone Mod]], [[Sable]]
