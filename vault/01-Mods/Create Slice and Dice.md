# Create: Slice & Dice

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/slice-and-dice)

## Role in Create Wars
Automates [[Farmer's Delight]] with Create:
- **Slicer** (cogwheel + andesite casing + turntable): holds a knife and runs cutting board recipes on
  items below it, like a press.
- **Sprinkler** (copper sheets + fluid pipe + iron bars, makes 3): sprays water to hydrate farmland, or
  liquid fertilizer to speed up crops.
- **Liquid Fertilizer**: mixed from organic compost and water in a basin.

## Dependencies
- Hard requires: [[Create]] `[6.0.9,7.0.0)` (pack has 6.0.10) and [[Kotlin for Forge]]
- Optional: [[Farmer's Delight]] (installed)

## Quests
Taught at the end of the **Food** chapter of the [[../03-Design/Quest Book|Quest Book]]. The Slicer quest
also needs Andesite Age Casing.
