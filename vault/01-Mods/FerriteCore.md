# FerriteCore

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/ferrite-core)

## Role in Create Wars
Memory optimization: eliminates duplicated block-state/model/other immutable-structure allocations.
Especially effective in packs with huge block/item counts — directly relevant here given 60+ mods each
adding their own blockstates (Create addons in particular multiply blockstate variants heavily).
