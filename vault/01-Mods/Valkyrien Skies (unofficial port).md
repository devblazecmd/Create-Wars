# Valkyrien Skies (unofficial NeoForge port)

**Status: rejected 2026-09-01** — superseded by [[Create Aeronautics]], which covers the vehicle/movement
role this would have filled. Kept for history per the taxonomy's `#mod/status/removed` convention; don't
re-add without a fresh discussion of what Aeronautics can't do.

**Source:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/valkyrien-skies-(unofficial-neoforge-port))

## Role in Create Wars
Physics engine that would let structures (ships, airships) move and rotate as rigid bodies — potential
basis for ship-to-ship combat. **This is an unofficial community port**, not the official Valkyrien Skies
2 mod: the official project does not (as of research time) support 1.21.1/NeoForge, and per the port's own
listing, bugs should be reported to the port author, not upstream VS devs.

## Dependencies
- Unknown — needs research before pinning. Likely has its own compat requirements with [[Create]] and
  [[Flywheel]] given rendering overlap.

## Version notes
Not pinned. Treat as higher-risk than the rest of the mod list: no official upstream support means slower
fixes on future MC version bumps, which directly cuts against the "easy to fix bugs on version updates"
goal for this pack. Recommend a deliberate go/no-go discussion before adding this, rather than including it
by default.
